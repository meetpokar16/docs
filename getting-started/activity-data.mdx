Rivlo needs access to your activity data to track your steps.

## iPhone

On iPhone, Rivlo uses **Apple Health**. Grant the required permissions so Rivlo can read supported activity data.

![Apple Health permission screen for Rivlo](/images/apple-health-1.png)

## Android

On Android, Rivlo uses **Health Connect**. Grant the required permissions so Rivlo can read supported activity data.

## After you connect

Once permissions are granted, Rivlo can read supported activity data and use it in the app. Your current step count appears on the Home screen, and previous activity remains available for review.

You can also record supported activities manually when that option is available.

## If steps look wrong

1. Confirm Rivlo has permission to access activity data.
2. Open Rivlo and check whether the Home screen updates.
3. Confirm your phone or watch is recording activity in Apple Health or Health Connect.

See [Why aren't my steps updating?](/troubleshooting/steps-not-updating).
