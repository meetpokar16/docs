The Home screen shows your current activity, daily goal, and important updates from Rivlo. Most of the information you need during the day is available here.

![Rivlo Home screen showing daily steps, goal progress, and streak](/images/home-screen.png)

## Daily progress

The main section shows your steps for the day and your progress toward your daily goal.

As your step count changes, your progress updates automatically. If you have a streak, you can also see it on the Home screen.

## Weekly activity

The weekly activity section shows your steps across the past seven days. Use it to compare recent days and see whether you have been reaching your daily goal.

## Home screen cards

Cards below your activity summary are shortcuts to other parts of Rivlo. Depending on what is available, you may see cards for:

- Goals
- Analytics
- Clubs
- League
- Other active Rivlo features

Select a card to open that feature. You do not need to open every card.

## What the numbers mean

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Steps** | Steps Rivlo has recorded for the day |
| **Goal** | Your daily step target |
| **Progress** | How close you are to your goal |
| **Streak** | Consecutive days you completed your daily goal |

## If your steps look wrong

If the Home screen does not match the activity on your phone or watch, check that Rivlo has permission to access your activity data.

See [Why aren't my steps updating?](/troubleshooting/steps-not-updating) and [How Rivlo gets your activity data](/getting-started/activity-data).
