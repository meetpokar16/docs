The Home screen shows your current activity, daily goal, and important updates from Rivlo.

Most of the information you need during the day is available here.

## Your daily progress

The main section shows your steps for the day and your progress toward your daily goal.

As your step count changes, your progress updates automatically.

If you have a streak, you can also see your current streak from the Home screen.

## Weekly activity

The weekly activity section shows your steps across the past seven days.

Use this section to compare your recent activity from one day to another.

It can also help you see whether you've been consistently reaching your daily goal.

## Home screen cards

The cards below your activity summary provide shortcuts to other parts of Rivlo.

Depending on the information available to you, you may see cards for:

  <Card title="Goals" icon="bullseye">

  <Card title="Analytics" icon="chart-line">
    Open a deeper view of your activity trends.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Clubs" icon="people-group">
    Check your Club and how the group is doing.
  </Card>

  <Card title="League" icon="ranking-star">

You may also see cards for other active Rivlo features. Select a card to open that feature.

<Tip>
  You don't need to open each card. They are shortcuts to information that may be useful to you.
</Tip>

## What the numbers mean

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Steps" icon="shoe-prints">
    Your **steps** are the number of steps Rivlo has recorded for the day.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Goal" icon="bullseye">
    Your **goal** is the number of steps you've set as your daily target.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Progress" icon="chart-simple">
    Your **progress** shows how close you are to reaching that goal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Streak" icon="fire">
    Your **streak** is the number of consecutive days you've completed your daily goal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## If your steps look wrong

If the step count on the Home screen doesn't match the activity recorded on your phone or watch, check that Rivlo has permission to access your activity data.

<Warning>
  Permissions must be granted for Rivlo to read supported activity data. Without them, your Home screen may show incomplete or outdated steps.
</Warning>

For help with permissions and activity data, see [How Rivlo works — What you need to use Rivlo](/getting-started/how-rivlo-works#what-you-need-to-use-rivlo).
