---

Every time you open Rivlo, the Home screen is the first thing you see — and it's designed to give you a complete picture of your day without any tapping around. From your current step count to your weekly movement trend to quick access for goals, clubs, and analytics, everything you need is surfaced in one place. The more you understand what each section shows, the more useful the Home screen becomes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read your step progress">
    The main progress display at the top of the Home screen shows today's total step count alongside a visual indicator of how close you are to your selected daily goal. The ring or progress bar updates in real time as you move, so a quick glance any time of day tells you exactly where you stand and how much further you need to go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your recent rhythm">
    Below your daily progress, the weekly overview shows a bar or summary of your movement across the past seven days. Use it to spot patterns — strong weekday consistency, a quiet weekend, a streak you're close to breaking. Seeing your movement as a trend, not just a single number, makes it easier to stay motivated and plan ahead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore what matters next">
    The lower section of the Home screen surfaces cards for the parts of Rivlo most relevant to you right now: your active goals, detailed analytics, Club activity, league standings, and more. Each card is a shortcut — tap it to go deeper into that feature without navigating through menus.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Tap any card on the Home screen to jump directly into that feature. It's the fastest way to check your league rank, see how your Club is performing in a War, or review your goal progress without hunting through the app.
</Tip>
