Rivlo is a social fitness app for walking, running, and hiking.

It tracks your activity, helps you build daily habits, and gives you ways to compete with friends and other Rivlo players. You can work toward personal goals, maintain streaks, join Clubs, and compete in leagues.

### Track your activity

Rivlo records your daily steps and activities so you can see how much you move each day.

Depending on your device and setup, Rivlo can use your health or activity data automatically. You can also record supported activities manually.

<Tip>
  Connect health or step tracking early so Rivlo can fill your Home screen without manual logging every day.
</Tip>

### Set goals and build streaks

Choose a daily step goal and work toward it each day.

When you reach your goal, Rivlo counts the day toward your streak. Your progress history also gives you a way to see how consistently you've been moving over time.

![Set Goals](/images/set-goals.png "Set Goals")

### Compete with other people

Rivlo is built around competition as well as personal progress.

You can compare your activity with friends, compete on leaderboards, and join Clubs. Clubs give you a group to compete and progress with.

For players who want a more competitive experience, Rivlo also has Club Wars and a league system. Your performance can move you through the leagues, from **Rookie** to **Immortal**.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are Clubs?" icon="users">
    Clubs are Rivlo's team feature. Join an existing group or create your own to move together, track contributions, and compete as a unit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are Club Wars?" icon="swords">
    Club Wars are live, time-limited step battles between two Clubs. Scoring, requirements, and battle status live in the Clubs experience.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do leagues work?" icon="ranking-star">
    Rivlo's league ladder rewards consistent movement. Climb from Rookie toward Immortal as your performance improves over time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Find your place in Rivlo

Rivlo gives you several ways to use the app depending on what motivates you.

You can use it simply to keep up with your daily step goal. Or you can use the competitive features to challenge friends, join a Club, and work your way up the leagues.

![Apple Health](/images/apple-health.png "Apple Health")

<Info>
  There isn't a separate mode you need to choose. Start by moving, and use the parts of Rivlo that are useful to you.
</Info>

## Getting started

The quickest way to start is to download Rivlo and connect your health or activity data.

Once your data is available, Rivlo can start showing your activity on the Home screen. From there, set your daily goal and explore the leaderboard or Clubs when you're ready.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Rivlo">
    Get Rivlo on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rivlo-steps-runs-hikes/id6756506796) or [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rivlo.app).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect health or activity data">
    Allow the permissions Rivlo needs so steps and supported activities can flow into the app.

    <Frame>
      ![Apple Health 1](/images/apple-health-1.png "Apple Health 1")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Home, then explore">
    Confirm today's activity on the Home screen, set a daily goal, and open leaderboards or Clubs when you're ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You don't need to configure everything before you start. Get your activity data connected first, then explore the rest of Rivlo as you go.
</Check>
