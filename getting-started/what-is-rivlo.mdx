Rivlo is a social fitness app for walking, running, and hiking.

It tracks your activity, helps you keep a daily step goal, and gives you ways to compete with friends and other Rivlo players. You can maintain streaks, join Clubs, compete in Club Wars, and progress through Leagues.

![Rivlo Home screen showing daily steps, goal progress, and streak](/images/home-screen.png)

## What you can do in Rivlo

### Track activity

Rivlo records your daily steps and supported activities. Depending on your device, Rivlo can use health or activity data automatically. You can also record supported activities manually.

See [How Rivlo gets your activity data](/getting-started/activity-data).

### Set goals and keep streaks

Choose a daily step goal. When you reach your goal, that day counts toward your streak. Rivlo also shows your longest streak.

See [How daily goals and streaks work](/activity-goals/goals-and-streaks).

### Compete with other people

You can compare activity with friends, use leaderboards, join Clubs, compete in Club Wars, and progress through Leagues from **Rookie** to **Immortal**.

You do not need to use every competitive feature. You can use Rivlo only for daily tracking if that is what you want.

## Getting started

1. Download Rivlo on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rivlo-steps-runs-hikes/id6756506796) or [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rivlo.app).
2. Connect your activity data (Apple Health on iPhone, or Health Connect on Android).
3. Check today's activity on the Home screen, set a daily goal, and explore other features when you are ready.

You do not need to configure everything before you start. Connect activity data first, then explore the rest of Rivlo as you go.
