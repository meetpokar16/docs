Rivlo is a social fitness app that transforms everyday movement into a shared experience. Whether you're hitting a step goal on your commute, logging a weekend hike, or racing up a league leaderboard, Rivlo ties your walks, runs, and hikes together into a clear picture of progress — with friends, clubs, and RIVO alongside you every step of the way.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Move through your day">
    Rivlo quietly records your activity in the background — steps, runs, and hikes — and turns everything into a clean, readable daily summary. Connect Apple Watch for automatic GPS tracking, or log activities manually whenever you prefer. At a glance, you always know where you stand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your momentum">
    Set a daily step goal that fits your lifestyle and watch your streak grow the longer you hit it. Your weekly shape view shows how your movement is trending over time, so you can spot patterns, celebrate strong weeks, and course-correct before a streak slips.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it social">
    Invite friends, compare daily steps on a shared leaderboard, and join a Club to train alongside like-minded movers. When you're ready for more, jump into Club Wars, climb through Rivlo's six competitive leagues — Rookie all the way to Immortal — and push your ranking further each week.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The fastest way to get started is to download Rivlo, allow Health or step-tracking permissions, and let the app populate your Home screen with today's data. You don't need to configure anything to start building a streak.
</Tip>
