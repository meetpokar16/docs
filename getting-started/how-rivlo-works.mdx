Rivlo uses your daily steps to track progress and power competitive features.

Your steps can update your daily goal, streak, friend comparisons, Club results, Club War scores, and League progression.

## Your steps

After you connect activity data, Rivlo reads your steps and updates your progress.

Your current step count appears on the Home screen. Rivlo also keeps previous activity so you can review how you have been doing over time.

See [How Rivlo gets your activity data](/getting-started/activity-data).

## Daily goals and streaks

You can set a daily step goal. When you reach your goal, that day counts toward your streak. Your streak is the number of consecutive days you completed your goal.

Your goal is personal. You can change it as your activity changes.

See [How daily goals and streaks work](/activity-goals/goals-and-streaks).

## Leaderboards

Leaderboards let you compare your steps with other Rivlo players, including friends.

## Clubs

A Club is a group of Rivlo players who compete together. After you join, you can see members and Club performance. Your activity can contribute to Club results and Club Wars.

See [What are Clubs?](/clubs-club-wars/what-are-clubs).

## Club Wars

Club Wars are competitions between two Clubs. During a War, members contribute eligible activity to their Club's score. The Club with the higher total wins.

See [How Club Wars work](/clubs-club-wars/how-club-wars-work).

## Leagues

Rivlo has a League system for longer-term competition. Performance during an Arc affects your League position.

League order:

**Rookie → Veteran → Diamond → Master → Grandmaster → Immortal**

Your League is separate from your daily step goal. You can use Rivlo without focusing on Leagues.

See [How Leagues work](/leagues-rankings/how-leagues-work).

## How the features connect

The different parts of Rivlo use the same activity data.

Your steps update daily progress. Daily progress can contribute to your streak. The same activity can also affect leaderboards, Club results, Club Wars, and League progression.

You do not need to use every feature.
