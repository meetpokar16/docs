Rivlo uses your daily steps to track your progress and power its competitive features.

Your steps are used in different parts of the app. You can work toward a daily goal, keep a streak, compare your activity with other players, compete with your Club, and move through the League system.

## Your steps

After you connect your activity data, Rivlo reads your steps and updates your progress.

Your current step count is shown on the Home screen. Rivlo also keeps your previous activity so you can check how you've been doing over time.

## Daily goals and streaks

You can set a daily step goal in Rivlo.

When you reach your goal, that day counts toward your streak. Your streak shows how many consecutive days you've completed your goal.

<Tip>
  Your goal is personal. You can change it as your activity changes.
</Tip>

## Leaderboards

Leaderboards let you compare your steps with other Rivlo players.

You can compete with your friends and see where you rank based on your activity.

## Clubs

A Club is a group of Rivlo players who compete together.

After joining a Club, you can see its members and its performance. Your activity contributes to your Club's results.

Clubs are also used for Club Wars, where two Clubs compete against each other.

## Club Wars

Club Wars are competitions between two Clubs.

During a War, members contribute their activity to their Club's score. The Club with the better result wins the competition.

<Info>
  The exact rules of a War can depend on the type of competition.
</Info>

## Leagues

Rivlo has a League system for longer-term competition.

Your performance determines your position in the League system. As you progress, you can move into higher Leagues.

## How the features connect

The different parts of Rivlo use the same activity data.

Your steps update your daily progress. Your daily progress contributes to your streak. Your activity can also affect your position on leaderboards, your Club's results, and your League progression.

## What you need to use Rivlo

Rivlo needs access to your activity data to track your steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iPhone">
    On iPhone, Rivlo uses **Apple Health**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    On Android, Rivlo uses **Health Connect**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Once you grant the required permissions, Rivlo can read the supported activity data and use it in the app.

For a quick start, see [What is Rivlo? — Getting started](/getting-started/what-is-rivlo#getting-started).
