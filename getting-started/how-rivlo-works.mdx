After you download Rivlo and grant step-tracking permissions, the app starts working immediately. Everything begins on your Home screen — a single view that captures today's movement at a glance — and expands outward as naturally or as competitively as you want to go. There's no complicated setup: Rivlo meets you where you are and grows with your goals.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your home">
    Your Home screen is the daily command center. It shows today's step count, how close you are to your goal, your current streak, and a snapshot of recent activity. Open Rivlo any time to get an instant read on where the day stands — no digging required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore your progress">
    Tap into activity views and analytics to see the rhythm behind your movement. Weekly trends reveal whether you're building consistency or trending downward, helping you make small adjustments before they turn into broken streaks. The more you move, the richer this picture gets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your next challenge">
    When you're ready to push further, Rivlo gives you options. Add friends and race them on a shared leaderboard. Join a Club and contribute to its collective step count. Enter a Club War and compete alongside your teammates for weekly glory. Or focus on the Leagues ladder — climb from Rookie through Veteran, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster, and all the way to Immortal at your own pace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Rivlo is available free on [iOS (App Store)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rivlo-steps-runs-hikes/id6756506796) and [Android (Google Play)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rivlo.app), with Apple Watch support for automatic activity and GPS tracking. Rivlo Pro (Monthly $4.99 / Yearly $34.99) unlocks additional features via in-app purchase.
</Info>
