Short definitions of Rivlo terms. Each term links to a more detailed page when one exists.

## A

**Arc**
A Rivlo season or competition period, such as a Summer Arc or Spring Arc. League position may be soft-reset when an Arc ends. See [How Leagues work](/leagues-rankings/how-leagues-work).

## C

**Club**
A group of Rivlo players who compete together. A Club can have up to 50 members. See [What are Clubs?](/clubs-club-wars/what-are-clubs).

**Club Leader**
The Club role with full management access, including starting Club Wars.

**Club War**
A competition between two Clubs. Members contribute eligible activity to their Club's score during the War. See [How Club Wars work](/clubs-club-wars/how-club-wars-work).

**Co-Leader**
A Club role that helps manage the Club and day-to-day Club activity.

## D

**Daily goal**
The number of steps you set as your daily target. See [How daily goals and streaks work](/activity-goals/goals-and-streaks).

**Diamond**
The third League tier. See [How Leagues work](/leagues-rankings/how-leagues-work).

## G

**Grandmaster**
The fifth League tier.

## I

**Immortal**
The highest League tier in Rivlo.

## L

**Leaderboard**
A ranking of players based on the activity or competition represented by that leaderboard. Separate from the League system.

**League**
Rivlo's six-tier progression system: Rookie, Veteran, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster, Immortal. See [How Leagues work](/leagues-rankings/how-leagues-work).

## M

**Master**
The fourth League tier.

## R

**RIVO**
Rivlo's AI activity coach. Available with Rivlo Pro. See [What is RIVO?](/rivo/what-is-rivo).

**Rivlo Pro**
Rivlo's paid subscription. Includes advanced analytics, Pro themes, RIVO, and related features. Pricing is shown in the app. See [Account and settings](/account-settings/overview).

**Rookie**
The first League tier.

## S

**Shadow**
A minimal Rivlo Home Screen widget. See [How Rivlo widgets work](/widgets-personalization/how-rivlo-widgets-work).

**Streak**
The number of consecutive days you completed your daily step goal. See [How daily goals and streaks work](/activity-goals/goals-and-streaks).

## V

**Veteran**
The second League tier.

## W

**War Ticket**
Used by a Club Leader to start a Club War. The number required depends on the War type. See [What are War Tickets?](/clubs-club-wars/what-are-war-tickets).
