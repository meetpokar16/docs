RIVO is Rivlo's AI activity coach.

You can ask RIVO questions about your activity and your progress in Rivlo. It can use information from your Rivlo account, such as your steps, goals, streaks, League position, and Club activity, to answer questions about your current progress.

RIVO is designed for questions that would otherwise require you to check several parts of the app yourself.

For example, instead of checking your step count, comparing it with last week, and working out how many more steps you need to reach your goal, you can ask RIVO directly.

## What does RIVO know?

RIVO can use relevant information from your Rivlo account when answering a question.

This can include:

- Your recent and historical activity
- Your daily step goals
- Your current and previous streaks
- Your League position
- Your Club activity
- Club War information
- Comparisons with other Rivlo players where the relevant data is available

RIVO uses this context to answer questions about your Rivlo activity.

You don't need to repeat information that is already available in your Rivlo account.

For example, you can ask:

> How am I doing this week?

RIVO can use your recent activity to answer the question instead of requiring you to provide your step counts yourself.

## What can you ask RIVO?

You can ask questions about your own activity, goals, competition, and progress.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Your activity" icon="shoe-prints">
    Ask RIVO to summarize or explain your activity.

    Examples:

    > How am I doing this week?

    > Am I walking more than last week?

    > What was my best day this month?

    > How many steps have I taken this week?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your daily goal" icon="bullseye">
    RIVO can help you understand your progress toward your daily goal.

    Examples:

    > How many steps do I need today to hit my goal?

    > Have I reached my goal today?

    > How many days did I reach my goal this week?

    > How is my current streak going?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your League" icon="ranking-star">
    You can ask about your current League position and your progress.

    Examples:

    > How am I doing in my League?

    > How close am I to Diamond?

    > How many steps do I need to reach the next League?

    > How does my progress compare with another player?

    The exact information RIVO can provide depends on the League data available in your account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your Club" icon="people-group">
    RIVO can also answer questions about your Club activity.

    Examples:

    > How is my Club doing?

    > Who is leading my Club?

    > How is our Club War going?

    > How much have I contributed to the Club this week?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Comparisons" icon="chart-simple">
    RIVO can compare activity when the required data is available.

    For example:

    > Compare my steps this month with Alex.

    > Am I walking more than Alex this week?

    > How many steps do I need to reach Diamond before Alex?

    These questions are useful when you want an answer based on more than one piece of information.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Ask follow-up questions

You don't have to start a new question every time.

RIVO can use the context of the conversation when you ask a follow-up.

For example:

> **You:** How many steps have I taken this week?
>
> **RIVO:** You've taken 62,400 steps this week.
>
> **You:** How does that compare with last week?

The second question can refer to the first answer without requiring you to repeat the date range or activity type.

You can continue the conversation as you would with a normal chat.

## How to get better answers

You don't need to use a specific command or format when talking to RIVO.

Ask your question naturally.

Specific questions usually produce more useful answers because they give RIVO a clear goal.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Less specific">
    > How am I doing?
  </Card>

  <Card title="More specific">
    > How am I doing with my steps this week compared with last week?
  </Card>
</Columns>

The second question gives RIVO a specific period and comparison to work with.

You can also ask RIVO to explain the result:

> Why am I behind last week?

Or continue with another question:

> What would I need to do to catch up?

## RIVO can explain your data

RIVO is not just a way to display another step count.

You can ask questions that require information from different parts of Rivlo.

For example:

> How many more steps do I need today to keep my streak and stay ahead of Alex?

A question like this can involve your daily goal, current activity, streak, and comparison with another player.

This is where RIVO can be useful when you don't want to work out the answer yourself.

## RIVO and Rivlo features

RIVO works with information from several parts of Rivlo.

| Rivlo feature | Example question |
| --- | --- |
| Steps | How many steps have I taken today? |
| Goals | How many steps do I need to reach my goal? |
| Streaks | How long is my current streak? |
| Leagues | How am I doing in my League? |
| Clubs | How is my Club doing? |
| Club Wars | How is our Club War going? |
| Friends | Compare my steps with Alex. |

The information available in an answer depends on the data currently available to RIVO.

## What RIVO doesn't replace

RIVO is a way to interact with your Rivlo data. It doesn't replace the underlying Rivlo features.

You can still use the app's normal screens to view your activity, check your League, see your Club, or review a Club War.

Use those screens when you want to inspect the underlying information directly. Use RIVO when you want to ask a question about that information.

## RIVO may not always have an answer

RIVO's answers depend on the information available in your Rivlo account and the features currently supported by RIVO.

If the required information isn't available, RIVO may not be able to answer the question.

For example, a question that requires activity data that Rivlo hasn't received can't produce a reliable activity comparison.

<Warning>
  If an answer doesn't look right, check the underlying Rivlo data first.
</Warning>

## RIVO is available with Rivlo Pro

RIVO is available as part of **Rivlo Pro**.

Upgrade to Rivlo Pro in the app to use RIVO and its activity insights, comparisons, and League-related guidance.

## Start using RIVO

Open the **RIVO** area in Rivlo and start with a question about your activity.

You can begin with something as simple as:

> How am I doing this week?

Then ask follow-up questions based on the answer.

<Check>
  You don't need to learn commands or a special format. Ask RIVO about your Rivlo activity in the same way you would ask another person.
</Check>
