RIVO (Rivlo Intelligence Virtual Object) is your AI-powered activity coach inside Rivlo, powered by Apple Intelligence. Instead of digging through stats yourself, you can ask RIVO a plain question and get a clear, contextual answer about your fitness progress, league standing, and club activity.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open RIVO">
    Head to the RIVO area in the app to start a conversation. RIVO has full context of your activity history, goals, streaks, and social progress — so the moment you open it, you're ready to get answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a direct question">
    Type naturally, like you would in a chat. Ask about your current week, today's step goal, your streak, your league ranking, or what you should focus on next. RIVO understands the context of your Rivlo account, so you don't need to explain the background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the context">
    RIVO's answers are designed to make your data easier to act on, not just easier to read. Whether you need a nudge to stay on track or want to compare your activity with a friend, RIVO gives you a clear picture and helps you decide what to do next.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Things You Can Ask RIVO

RIVO is built for natural, specific questions. Here are some examples to get you started:

- "How am I doing this week?"
- "How many steps do I need today to hit my goal?"
- "Am I walking more than last week?"
- "Compare my steps last month with Alex."
- "How many steps do I need to reach Diamond before Alex?"
- "How am I doing in my league?"
- "How is my Club War going?"

The more specific your question, the more useful RIVO's answer. You can ask follow-ups just like a normal conversation.

<Info>
  RIVO is available with **Rivlo Pro**. Upgrade in the app to unlock your AI coach and get full access to activity insights, comparisons, and league guidance.
</Info>
