RIVO is Rivlo's AI activity coach.

You can ask RIVO questions about your activity and progress in Rivlo. It can use information from your Rivlo account, such as steps, goals, streaks, League position, and Club activity, to answer questions about your current progress.

RIVO is useful for questions that would otherwise require checking several parts of the app yourself.

![RIVO start screen in Rivlo showing Ask RIVO and suggested actions](https://rivlo.3bytes.org/guide-assets/rivo.png)

## What RIVO can use

RIVO can use relevant information from your Rivlo account, including:

- Recent and historical activity
- Daily step goals
- Current and previous streaks
- League position
- Club activity
- Club War information
- Comparisons with other Rivlo players when the required data is available

You do not need to repeat information already available in your Rivlo account.

## What RIVO does not replace

RIVO does not replace Rivlo's normal screens. Use the app screens when you want to inspect the underlying information directly. Use RIVO when you want to ask a question about that information.

## Availability

RIVO is available as part of **Rivlo Pro**. Upgrade in the app to use RIVO.

## Start using RIVO

Open the **RIVO** area in Rivlo and ask a question about your activity, for example:

> How am I doing this week?

Then ask follow-up questions based on the answer. You do not need special commands or formats.
