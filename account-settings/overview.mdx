Manage your Rivlo account, subscription, notifications, and privacy settings all from within the app. Whether you're upgrading to Pro, adjusting what data you share, or personalising your experience with themes, everything is a few taps away in the Settings screen.

## What's in Settings

- **Sign in with Apple** — Rivlo uses Sign in with Apple for secure, password-free authentication. Your Apple ID keeps your account safe without creating a separate login.
- **Rivlo Pro** — Upgrade to Pro to unlock advanced analytics, an ad-free experience, exclusive themes, a Pro badge, and hourly step tracking. See the section below for details.
- **Privacy settings** — Control what activity and profile information is visible to other users, including friends and club members.
- **Notification preferences** — Choose which alerts you receive — daily goal reminders, streak warnings, challenge updates, league promotions, and more.
- **Theme selection** — Personalise the look of the app with Rivlo's built-in themes. Pro subscribers get access to an expanded set of exclusive themes.
- **App preferences** — Adjust units (steps, km/miles), step goal targets, connected health platforms, and other app-wide defaults.

## Rivlo Pro

Rivlo Pro gives you a deeper view of your activity and removes distractions so you can focus on your goals. A Pro subscription includes:

- **Advanced insights** — Weekly, monthly, and yearly activity breakdowns, hourly step tracking, Activity with Time analytics, and yearly heatmaps.
- **Ad-free experience** — Browse leagues, clubs, and your stats without interruption.
- **Exclusive themes** — Access a curated set of Pro-only themes to make the app feel like yours.
- **Pro badge** — A visible badge on your profile that shows your commitment to the community.

Pricing is shown in the app at the time of purchase. Both monthly and yearly plans are available.
