The **Account** screen is where you manage your Rivlo account, subscription, themes, widgets, health permissions, and app preferences.

Open **Account** from the bottom navigation in Rivlo.

![Rivlo Account settings showing Rivlo Pro, Themes, Widgets, Health Permissions, and Units](https://rivlo.3bytes.org/guide-assets/settings.png)

## Account

Rivlo uses **Sign in with Apple** for authentication. Account settings control how you sign in and manage your profile.

## Rivlo Pro

You can manage your Rivlo Pro subscription from Account. Rivlo Pro includes features such as:

- Advanced activity analytics
- Hourly step tracking
- Yearly activity heatmaps
- An ad-free experience
- Pro-only themes
- A Pro badge

Pricing and available plans are shown in the app.

RIVO is also available with Rivlo Pro. See [What is RIVO?](/rivo/what-is-rivo).

## Privacy

Privacy settings control which parts of your Rivlo activity and profile information other users can see, including friends, Club members, and other Rivlo users.

Account also includes **RIVO Availability & Privacy** settings for RIVO.

## Notifications

Notification settings control which alerts Rivlo can send. Depending on your account, these can include:

- Daily goal reminders
- Streak reminders
- Challenge updates
- League updates
- Club activity

## Themes

Themes change the appearance of Rivlo. See [Rivlo themes](/widgets-personalization/themes).

## App preferences

App preferences can include:

- Step and distance units
- Daily step goal
- Health permissions
- Connected wearables through Apple Health
- Home Screen widgets

Available settings can depend on your device and account features.

See [How Rivlo gets your activity data](/getting-started/activity-data) and [How Rivlo widgets work](/widgets-personalization/how-rivlo-widgets-work).
