## Account

Your account settings control how you sign in to Rivlo and manage your account.

Rivlo uses **Sign in with Apple** for authentication.

## Rivlo Pro

You can manage your Rivlo Pro subscription from Settings.

Rivlo Pro includes additional features such as:

- Advanced activity analytics
- Hourly step tracking
- Yearly activity heatmaps
- An ad-free experience
- Pro-only themes
- A Pro badge

Pricing and available plans are shown in the app.

<Card title="Activity analytics" icon="chart-line" href="/activity-goals/overview#analytics">
  See how Pro analytics fit into Activity & Goals.
</Card>

## Privacy

Privacy settings control which parts of your Rivlo activity and profile information other users can see.

Depending on the setting, you can control visibility for information shared with friends, Club members, and other Rivlo users.

## Notifications

Notification settings control which alerts Rivlo can send you.

Depending on the features available on your account, notifications can include:

- Daily goal reminders
- Streak reminders
- Challenge updates
- League updates
- Club activity

You can change your notification preferences from Settings.

## Themes

Themes change the appearance of Rivlo.

Rivlo includes built-in themes, with additional themes available to Pro subscribers.

Your selected theme can also affect supported Rivlo widgets.

<Card title="Change your theme" icon="palette" href="/widgets-personalization/how-rivlo-widgets-work#change-your-rivlo-theme">
  See the built-in themes and how they apply to widgets.
</Card>

## App preferences

App preferences control settings that affect how Rivlo works.

These can include:

- Step and distance units
- Daily step goal
- Connected health platforms
- Other app-wide preferences

The settings available to you can depend on your device and the features enabled on your account.

<Info>
  For connecting activity data, see [How Rivlo works — What you need to use Rivlo](/getting-started/how-rivlo-works#what-you-need-to-use-rivlo).
</Info>

## Where to find Settings

Open Rivlo and open **Settings** from your profile or account area.
