The **Settings** screen is where you manage your Rivlo account, subscription, privacy, notifications, and app preferences.

Open Rivlo and open **Settings** from your profile or account area. The exact location can vary between app versions.

## Account

Rivlo uses **Sign in with Apple** for authentication. Account settings control how you sign in and manage your account.

## Rivlo Pro

You can manage your Rivlo Pro subscription from Settings. Rivlo Pro includes features such as:

- Advanced activity analytics
- Hourly step tracking
- Yearly activity heatmaps
- An ad-free experience
- Pro-only themes
- A Pro badge

Pricing and available plans are shown in the app.

RIVO is also available with Rivlo Pro. See [What is RIVO?](/rivo/what-is-rivo).

## Privacy

Privacy settings control which parts of your Rivlo activity and profile information other users can see, including friends, Club members, and other Rivlo users.

## Notifications

Notification settings control which alerts Rivlo can send. Depending on your account, these can include:

- Daily goal reminders
- Streak reminders
- Challenge updates
- League updates
- Club activity

## Themes

Themes change the appearance of Rivlo. Built-in themes include Rivlo Blue, Dark, Monochrome, Gold, and Sunfire. Additional themes may be available with Rivlo Pro. Your selected theme can also affect supported widgets.

See [How Rivlo widgets work](/widgets-personalization/how-rivlo-widgets-work#themes).

## App preferences

App preferences can include:

- Step and distance units
- Daily step goal
- Connected health platforms
- Other app-wide preferences

Available settings can depend on your device and account features.

See [How Rivlo gets your activity data](/getting-started/activity-data).
