Leagues are Rivlo's progression system. Your activity and performance determine your position in the League system.

There are six Leagues:

1. Rookie
2. Veteran
3. Diamond
4. Master
5. Grandmaster
6. Immortal

## Your League

Your current League shows where you are in Rivlo's progression system.

As you compete and record activity, your performance affects your position. The goal is to progress through the Leagues and reach a higher tier.

Each League represents a higher position in the progression system.

## How you progress

Your recorded activity and performance contribute to your League progression.

Keep participating in Rivlo's competitive features and maintain your activity throughout the Arc.

Your League position is based on your performance within the current Arc.

## Arcs

Rivlo uses **Arcs** for its seasons.

An Arc is a defined competition period, such as a Summer Arc or Spring Arc.

Your League position may be adjusted when an Arc ends. This gives each new Arc its own progression period.

<Info>
  Your previous results still remain part of your Rivlo history, but your position for the new Arc may not be the same as where you finished the previous Arc.
</Info>

## What happens when an Arc ends?

When an Arc ends, Rivlo processes the results from that Arc and starts the next one.

Your League position may be soft-reset for the new Arc. You then compete again during the new Arc to progress through the League system.

The exact reset can depend on the current League rules.

## League order

The League progression is:

**Rookie → Veteran → Diamond → Master → Grandmaster → Immortal**

Immortal is the highest League in Rivlo.
