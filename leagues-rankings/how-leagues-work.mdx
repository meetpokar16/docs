Rivlo Leagues are a six-tier progression system that rewards consistent movement. As you log walks, runs, and hikes, your performance shapes where you land in the rankings — pushing you toward the next tier and keeping the competition meaningful.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start in your current League">
    Your League represents where you are on Rivlo's progression path right now. It frames your next milestone and shows you exactly what you're working toward. Every player starts somewhere — and every tier is a step closer to the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep building consistent movement">
    Your recorded activity and performance shape your progression over time. There are no shortcuts — regular movement is what moves the needle. Stay active, hit your daily goals, and maintain your streak to build upward momentum.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Climb to the next level">
    The path runs from Rookie all the way to Immortal, with each tier representing a higher level of consistency and performance. Reach the top of one tier and the next opens up. Every Arc is a fresh chance to push higher.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## League Tiers

Rivlo has six tiers, each representing a milestone in your fitness journey.

| Tier | What it means |
|---|---|
| 🟤 **Rookie** | Your starting point. Build your foundation, establish habits, and find your rhythm. |
| 🔵 **Veteran** | You've found your stride. Consistent movers who show up day after day. |
| 💎 **Diamond** | Dedicated athletes climbing the rankings with commitment and focus. |
| 🔴 **Master** | Elite performers with strong streaks and a proven record of activity. |
| 🟣 **Grandmaster** | Top-tier competitors setting the pace for everyone below them. |
| 🟡 **Immortal** | The pinnacle of Rivlo. Only the most consistent, dedicated movers reach here. |

<Tip>
  Seasons in Rivlo are called **Arcs** (like the Summer Arc or Spring Arc). Your league position may soft-reset between Arcs, so each new season is a fresh opportunity to climb — no matter where you finished before.
</Tip>
