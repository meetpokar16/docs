Leagues are Rivlo's progression system. Your activity and performance determine your position in the League system.

There are six Leagues:

1. Rookie
2. Veteran
3. Diamond
4. Master
5. Grandmaster
6. Immortal

## Your League

Your current League shows where you are in Rivlo's progression system. As you compete and record activity, your performance affects your position.

## League tiers

| League | Position |
| --- | --- |
| Rookie | 1 |
| Veteran | 2 |
| Diamond | 3 |
| Master | 4 |
| Grandmaster | 5 |
| Immortal | 6 |

League order:

**Rookie → Veteran → Diamond → Master → Grandmaster → Immortal**

Immortal is the highest League.

## How you progress

Your recorded activity and performance contribute to League progression within the current Arc.

Rivlo does not publish exact promotion thresholds in this Guide. Your in-app League screen shows your current position and related progress information.

## Arcs

Rivlo uses **Arcs** for its seasons. An Arc is a defined competition period, such as a Summer Arc or Spring Arc.

When an Arc ends, Rivlo processes that Arc's results and starts the next one. Your League position may be soft-reset for the new Arc. Previous results remain part of your Rivlo history, but your starting position for the new Arc may differ.

The exact reset can depend on the current League rules.

## League vs. leaderboards

| Feature | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **League** | Progression through Rivlo's six-tier League system across Arcs |
| **Leaderboard** | Ranking for a specific activity or competition |

You can use both to track performance.

## FAQ

### What is the highest League?

Immortal.

### Do Leagues reset?

League positions may be soft-reset when an Arc ends.

### Can I lose my League?

Your position can change between Arcs depending on League reset rules.

### How do I reach a higher League?

Continue recording activity and taking part in Rivlo's competitive features. Performance within the current Arc determines progression.
