Rivlo widgets let you see selected Rivlo information from your iPhone Home Screen without opening the app.

You can add a widget for your daily activity, League status, League progress, or activity history. Rivlo also includes a minimal widget called **Shadow**.

Widgets update during the day as your Rivlo data changes.

## Available widgets

Rivlo currently includes these widgets:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Daily Activity" icon="shoe-prints">
    Shows your current step count and progress toward your daily goal.

    Use this widget if you want to check your activity without opening Rivlo.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="League Status" icon="trophy">
    Shows your current League and ranking.

    Use this widget to keep your current League position visible on your Home Screen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="League Progress" icon="chart-line">
    Shows your progress toward the next League tier.

    This widget is useful if you want to track your current position within the League system.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Activity Heatmap" icon="table-cells">
    Shows your activity over the past 30 days as a heatmap.

    It gives you a quick view of how active you have been across the month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Shadow" icon="moon">
    Shadow is a minimal Rivlo widget that displays your activity without showing as much information as the other widgets.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Widget sizes

Rivlo widgets are available in different sizes.

When you add a Rivlo widget, iOS shows the sizes available for that widget. The information displayed can vary by widget and size.

Choose the size that fits the amount of information you want to see.

## Add a Rivlo widget

To add a Rivlo widget to your iPhone Home Screen:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter jiggle mode">
    Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen until the apps start to jiggle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the widget picker">
    Tap the **\+** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Rivlo">
    Search for **Rivlo**, then select **Rivlo** from the widget list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose widget and size">
    Browse the available widgets, choose one, and select the widget size.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add and place">
    Tap **Add Widget**, move the widget to the position you want, then tap **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After you add the widget, Rivlo updates the displayed information as your activity changes.

## Change a widget

You can change the widget's configuration after adding it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open widget options">
    Touch and hold the Rivlo widget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit settings">
    Tap **Edit Widget** if the option is available, then change the available settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close">
    Close the widget settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The options available depend on the widget.

## Change your Rivlo theme

Rivlo includes built-in themes that change the appearance of the app and its widgets.

The available themes are:

| Theme | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Rivlo Blue** | The default Rivlo theme |
| **Dark** | A dark theme |
| **Monochrome** | A black-and-white theme |
| **Gold** | A warm theme |
| **Sunfire** | A brighter, more colorful theme |

To change your theme, open **Settings** in Rivlo and find the **Appearance** or **Theme** setting.

Your selected theme also applies to Rivlo widgets.

## Widget data

Widgets display information from Rivlo.

If a widget is not showing the latest information, open Rivlo and check whether your activity has been updated there first.

Widget updates can also depend on iOS and the system's widget update behavior.

## If a widget is missing

If you cannot find Rivlo in the iOS widget picker:

1. Make sure Rivlo is installed on your iPhone.
2. Open Rivlo once.
3. Return to the Home Screen and open the widget picker again.
4. Search for **Rivlo**.

If the widget is already on your Home Screen but is not showing current information, open Rivlo and check your activity data.

If the problem continues, see [How Rivlo works — What you need to use Rivlo](/getting-started/how-rivlo-works#what-you-need-to-use-rivlo).
