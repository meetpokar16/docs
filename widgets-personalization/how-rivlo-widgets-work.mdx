Rivlo offers Home Screen Widgets so your step count, league status, and activity are always visible — even when the app isn't open. Pick the widget that matches what you care about most, drop it on your Home Screen, and stay on top of your progress throughout the day.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a Widget">
    Rivlo has several widget types to choose from. Think about what you want at a glance: today's step count, your current league position, a 30-day activity overview, or something else. Each widget is designed for a specific view of your progress, so pick the one that fits your daily routine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to your Home Screen">
    Long-press your iPhone Home Screen to enter jiggle mode, then tap the **+** button in the top-left corner to open the widget picker. Search for **Rivlo**, select it, and browse the available widgets. Choose your preferred size — small, medium, or large — then tap **Add Widget** to place it. Drag it to the position you want and tap **Done**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep progress close">
    Once your widget is live, your selected Rivlo data updates automatically throughout the day. Check your step count, league status, or activity heatmap with a single glance — no need to unlock the app. It's the fastest way to stay aware of your movement and keep momentum going.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  On iPhone, long-press the Home Screen and tap the **+** button to open the widget picker. Search for **Rivlo** to see all available widgets and size options.
</Tip>

## Available Widgets

Rivlo includes a range of widgets to match different ways of tracking progress.

- **Daily Activity** — Your step count and daily goal progress at a glance.
- **League Status** — See your current league tier and ranking without opening the app.
- **League Progress** — A visual breakdown of how close you are to the next league tier.
- **Activity Heatmap** — A 30-day intensity view showing your movement patterns over the past month.
- **Shadow** — A minimal, ambient widget that shows your activity in a subtle, glanceable format.

## App Themes

Rivlo lets you personalize the app's look with built-in themes. Choose the one that fits your style and it applies across the app — including your widgets.

| Theme | Style |
|---|---|
| **Rivlo Blue** | The default Rivlo look — clean and bold. |
| **Dark** | A deep, low-light theme for night-time use. |
| **Monochrome** | Stripped-back black and white. |
| **Gold** | A warm, premium feel. |
| **Sunfire** | Vibrant and energetic. |

To change your theme, open **Settings** in Rivlo and look for the **Appearance** or **Theme** section.
