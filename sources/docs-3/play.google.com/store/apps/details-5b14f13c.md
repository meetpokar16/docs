STOP WALKING ALONE. START COMPETING with RIVLO. 
Turn every step into a victory. Rivlo is more than just a free pedometer step counter, it’s a global arena where your movement earns you rank, trophies, and glory. Whether you are chasing step goals for weight loss or leading your team to victory in Club Wars, Rivlo transforms fitness into a social adventure. 

WHY RIVLO? 
While other steps apps just count numbers, Rivlo builds communities. Track your activity, Join Clubs with a sleek, battery-efficient social step tracker and stay motivated through competitive leagues designed to keep you moving every single day. 

NEW: CLUBS & CLUB WARS 
• Tactical Leadership: Build your legacy as a Leader or Co-Leader. Manage your club of up to 50 members and lead them into high-stakes competition. 
• The War Ticket System: Use War Tickets to initiate a challenge. Scour the Suggested Opponents list to find a rival club and declare war to prove your dominance. 
• Global War Leaderboard: It’s not just about walking; it’s about winning. Compete for the top spot on the exclusive Wars Won ranking and gain global recognition. 
• Head-to-Head Battles: Engage in direct competition where your club's total steps are pitted against the enemy in real-time. 
• Strategic Coordination: Use Club Chat and Club Invites to recruit the best athletes and coordinate your team’s efforts during active wars. 
• Anti-Cheat Protection: Every competition is kept fair with our robust anti-cheat mechanism, ensuring that only real effort leads to victory. 

COMPETE & CONQUER 
• Global Ranking System: Rise from Rookie to Immortal and earn exclusive trophies and badges as you hit your milestones. 
• Club Wars & Chat: Join a Club, invite friends, and compete in club wars. Strategize in real-time to climb the global rankings. 
• Step Challenges: Start friendly challenges, maintain your streaks, and see where you stand on the Global Leaderboard. 

ADVANCED HIKE & RUN TRACKING 
• Real-Time Hike Tracking: Take Rivlo outdoors. Track real-world hikes with live GPS, Pace, Elevation, and Altitude. 
• Run Tracking: Push your limits, track runs with detailed metrics and insights including Splits, Distance, and Route Mapping. 
• Performance Metrics: Monitor your Average Pace, hit your Best Pace, and stay on top of your Weekly Distance Covered to crush your running goals. 
• Hike/Run Leaderboards: Compete on specialized Hike and Run Leaderboards to prove your endurance and climb the global ranks. 
• Virtual Hikes: Explore the world through Rivlo Adventures and earn progress as you step, even when you aren't outdoors. 

DEEP HEALTH & BODY INSIGHTS 
• Body Goal Tracking: New features to set, track, and achieve your specific body goals and weight loss goals. 
• Total Tracking: Monitor your distance and calorie tracker in one beautiful dashboard. 

RIVLO PRO: UNLEASH THE POWER 
Take your fitness journey to the next level with our premium Pro features: 
• Advanced Insights: Deep analysis with weekly, monthly, and yearly overviews to visualize your progress over time. 
• Smart Analytics: Hourly step tracking and "Activity with Time" precision. 
• Yearly Heat Maps: A stunning visual representation of your consistency. 
• Ad-Free Experience: Focus on your health without interruptions. 
• Distinguished Status: Stand out with a Pro profile badge and exclusive Mono Chrome Themes. 

PERSONALIZED FOR YOU 
• Customizable Themes: Match the app to your style, including the new minimalist Mono Chrome look. 
• Home Screen Widgets: Keep your step tracker front and center. Check progress at a glance. 
• Social Community: Share your progress and stay inspired by a global network of athletes. 
Download Rivlo today and make every step count. Perfect for weight loss, hiking enthusiasts, step challenges, and anyone ready to make fitness fun again.
