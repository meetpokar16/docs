STOP WALKING ALONE. START COMPETING WITH RIVLO. Turn every step into progress. Rivlo is a competitive step tracker and pedometer that transforms daily movement into rankings, challenges, leagues, clubs, and motivation. Track your steps, hit step goals, compete with friends, climb leaderboards, and make walking more rewarding every day. WHY RIVLO? Most step tracking apps simply count numbers. Rivlo gives your steps somewhere to go. Use a sleek, battery-efficient social step tracker to follow your activity, build consistency, join a community, and compete through leagues designed to keep you moving. Meet RIVO — Rivlo Intelligence Virtual Object, your AI-powered activity coach inside Rivlo. Powered by Apple Intelligence, RIVO turns your step tracking data into personalized answers. Ask about daily steps, step goals, walking progress, activity trends, friends, rankings, leagues, leaderboards, challenges, Club Wars, and more through natural conversation. RIVO can compare your activity with friends and help you understand what it may take to stay ahead, climb the rankings, or reach your next league first. Ask RIVO: • “How am I doing this week?” • “How many steps do I need today?” • “Am I walking more than last week?” • “Compare my steps last month with Alex.” • “How many steps do I need to reach Diamond before Alex?” • “How am I doing in my league?” • “How is my Club War going?” Instead of searching through your step tracker, pedometer stats, activity history, friend activity, and competition screens, simply ask RIVO. Rivlo Intelligence helps you understand your activity, compare progress, analyze competition, and know what to do next. Your steps. Your progress. Your competition. One conversation. CLUBS & CLUB WARS Build or join a Club and compete together. • Create Clubs with up to 50 members • Assign Leaders and Co-Leaders • Use War Tickets to challenge rival Clubs • Compete head-to-head using total Club steps • Track Wars Won on the global leaderboard • Chat, invite friends, and coordinate your team • Anti-cheat protection helps keep battles fair COMPETE & CLIMB • Global Ranking System: Rise from Rookie to Veteran, Diamond, Master, Grand Master, and Immortal. • Step Challenges: Challenge friends, maintain streaks, and stay motivated. • Global Leaderboards: See how your daily steps compare with the Rivlo community. RUN & HIKE TRACKING Take Rivlo beyond daily walking. • Track outdoor hikes with GPS, pace, elevation, and altitude • Track runs with splits, distance, pace, and route mapping • Monitor average pace, best pace, and weekly distance • Compete on dedicated run and hike leaderboards • Explore Virtual Hikes through Rivlo Adventures HEALTH, ACTIVITY & BODY GOALS • Set and follow body goals and weight loss goals • Monitor distance, calories, daily activity, and progress RIVLO PRO • Weekly, monthly, and yearly activity insights • Hourly step tracking and Activity with Time analytics • Yearly heat maps to visualize consistency • Ad-free experience • Pro profile badge and exclusive Themes PERSONALIZED FOR YOU • Customizable themes to match your style • Home Screen Widgets for steps, leagues, activity, and progress • Social community features to stay connected and motivated Whether you want a step tracker, pedometer, walking tracker, step counter, step challenge app, run tracker, or hike tracker, Rivlo helps make movement more motivating.
