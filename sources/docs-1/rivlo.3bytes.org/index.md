GOT QUESTIONS?

## A few things before your first step.

What is Rivlo?+

Rivlo is a social step app built around leagues, trophies, global rankings, clubs, Club Wars, widgets, and RIVO AI.

Is Rivlo free to use?+

Yes. You can download Rivlo and begin building your progress for free.

Can I use Rivlo with friends?+

Yes. Create or join a club, invite friends, and compete together in Club Wars.

Is Android supported?+

Yes. Rivlo is available on Android via [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rivlo.app), and on iOS via the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rivlo-steps-runs-hikes/id6756506796).
