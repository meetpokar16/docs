Friends and competition features let you compare activity with other Rivlo players.

## What you can do

After you connect with a friend, you can:

- Compare daily steps
- Send step challenges
- Chat
- Compare League progress
- Ask RIVO to compare your activity

## Add friends

You add friends by searching for their Rivlo username and sending a friend request. They must accept before you are connected.

See [How to add friends](/friends-competition/how-to-add-friends).

## Leaderboards

Leaderboards let you compare your steps with other Rivlo players, including friends. Leaderboards are separate from the League system.

See [How Leagues work](/leagues-rankings/how-leagues-work) for the difference between Leagues and leaderboards.
