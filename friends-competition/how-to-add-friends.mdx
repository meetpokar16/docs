Adding friends on Rivlo lets you compare steps, challenge each other, and climb the leaderboard together. Once someone accepts your request, their activity shows up alongside yours — keeping things competitive and motivating every day.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the social area">
    In Rivlo, tap the **Friends** or **Competition** tab at the bottom of the screen. This is where you can search for other Rivlo users and manage your existing connections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for a person">
    Type their Rivlo username into the search bar. Check the profile that appears to confirm it's the right person before sending a request — usernames are unique, so an exact match means you've found them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your friend request">
    Tap **Add Friend** on their profile. Once they accept, their daily activity becomes visible to you and shared competition features — like step challenges and leaderboard comparisons — unlock automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Search by exact Rivlo username for the best results. Usernames are case-sensitive.
</Tip>

## What You Can Do With Friends

Once you're connected, a range of social and competitive features open up:

- **Compare daily steps** — See how your step count stacks up against friends on the leaderboard each day.
- **Send step challenges** — Invite a friend to a head-to-head step challenge and keep each other accountable.
- **Chat** — Send messages directly to friends inside the app (introduced in v4.1.8).
- **See each other's league progress** — Follow friends as they move through leagues from Rookie all the way up to Immortal.
- **Ask RIVO to compare your activity** — Your AI coach RIVO can pull up a side-by-side breakdown of your activity versus a friend's, so you always know where you stand.
