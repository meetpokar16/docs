You can add other Rivlo users by searching for their username.

## Add a friend

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ranks">
    Open **Ranks** from the bottom navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Friends">
    Select the **Friends** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search by username">
    Open the search field and enter the person's Rivlo username.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the request">
    Select the **\+** button next to their profile to send a friend request. The other person needs to accept it before you are connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![Rivlo Ranks screen with Friends tab and Add Friend search for a username](https://rivlo.3bytes.org/guide-assets/add-friends.png)
</Frame>

## After they accept

Once your friend accepts the request, you can see their activity and use social features between friends. You can:

- Compare daily steps
- Send step challenges
- Chat with friends
- Compare League progress
- Ask RIVO to compare your activity

See [What is RIVO?](/rivo/what-is-rivo).

## If you can't find someone

Search for their exact Rivlo username. If the profile does not appear, check the spelling and try again.
