You can add other Rivlo users by searching for their username.

## Add a friend

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Friends or Competition">
    Open **Friends** or **Competition** from the bottom navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search by username">
    Open the search field and enter the person's Rivlo username.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select their profile">
    Select their profile from the search results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the request">
    Select **Add Friend**.

    The request is sent immediately. The other person needs to accept it before you are connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After they accept

Once your friend accepts the request, you can see their activity and use the social features available between friends.

You can:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Compare daily steps" icon="shoe-prints">
    See how your step counts stack up.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Send step challenges" icon="bolt">
    Invite a friend to a head-to-head challenge.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Chat" icon="comments">
    Message friends inside Rivlo.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Compare League progress" icon="ranking-star">
    Follow each other's League standing.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Ask RIVO to compare" icon="robot" href="/rivo/what-is-rivo">
    Get a side-by-side activity comparison from RIVO.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## If you can't find someone

Make sure you are searching for their exact Rivlo username.

If the profile does not appear, check the spelling and try the search again.

<Tip>
  Exact username matches work best. Double-check spelling before you search again.
</Tip>
