If your Rivlo step count looks wrong or is not updating, check permissions and the activity data sources Rivlo uses.

## Possible causes

- Rivlo does not have permission to read activity data
- Apple Health or Health Connect is not recording activity
- Rivlo has not refreshed since new activity was recorded
- The activity was not accepted by Rivlo's activity validation

## What to check

### 1. Confirm permissions

- On iPhone, Rivlo uses **Apple Health**
- On Android, Rivlo uses **Health Connect**

Make sure Rivlo has permission to access supported activity data.

See [How Rivlo gets your activity data](/getting-started/activity-data).

### 2. Confirm the phone or watch is recording activity

Open Apple Health or Health Connect and check whether steps are being recorded there.

If the system health app has no steps, Rivlo cannot show those steps.

### 3. Open Rivlo

Open Rivlo and check the Home screen. Widgets and other screens may lag behind the in-app Home screen.

### 4. Check competitive features separately

If steps appear on Home but not in a Club War, only eligible activity recorded during the active War window counts. See [How Club Wars work](/clubs-club-wars/how-club-wars-work).

## If it still doesn't work

1. Revoke and re-grant activity permissions, then open Rivlo again.
2. Restart your phone.
3. Confirm you are signed in to the correct Rivlo account.

For account and permission settings, see [Account and settings](/account-settings/overview).
