---
title: "Rivlo War Tickets: How to Enter and Start a Club War"
sidebarTitle: "War Tickets"
description: "War Tickets are how your Club initiates a Club War challenge. Learn where to find them, how to check requirements, and what they're used for."
---

War Tickets are the entry mechanism for Club Wars. A club needs Tickets to initiate or enter a Club War — they're what your Club Leader uses to formally declare a challenge against a rival and get the battle started. Without a Ticket, a War can't be initiated, so keeping track of your Club's supply matters.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find Your Club War Area">
    Head to your Club's War area inside the app. This is your central hub for all things Club Wars — you'll see any currently active War, available War options, your Club's War history, and the current status of any ongoing event. If your Club Leader has already initiated a War, the active battle will be displayed here with live score tracking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Ticket Requirements">
    Before a War can be started, check the event details in the War area for any Ticket requirement or participation rule. Some Wars may require one or more Tickets to initiate — the specifics depend on the event type. Reviewing the requirements before declaring a challenge helps you avoid surprises and ensures your Club is ready to compete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Tickets to Initiate a War">
    When your Club is ready to compete, the Club Leader can browse the Suggested Opponents list to find a rival club and declare war. If the event requires Tickets, they are used at the point of initiation — unlocking your Club's ability to enter that War. Once a challenge is declared and accepted, the War window opens and every member's movement starts counting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  War Ticket requirements may vary by event type — always check the full event details in your Club's War area before initiating a challenge so you know exactly what's needed.
</Info>
