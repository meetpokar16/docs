A War Ticket is used by a Club to start a Club War.

Only the Club Leader can use a War Ticket to start a War. The number of Tickets required depends on the type of War.

## How War Tickets work

War Tickets are used when your Club starts a War against another Club.

To start a War:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the War area">
    Open your Club's **War** area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check requirements">
    Check the available War and its requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an opponent">
    Choose a Club from the available opponents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the War">
    Start the War. If the War requires a Ticket, it is used when the challenge is started.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the challenge is accepted, the War begins and eligible activity starts counting toward the Club's score.

## How many Tickets do you need?

The Ticket requirement depends on the type of War.

Check the War details before starting a challenge to see how many Tickets are required.

<Tip>
  Always review the War details in your Club's War area before starting a challenge.
</Tip>

## Who can use a War Ticket?

Only the **Club Leader** can start a War and use the Club's War Tickets.

Club members can participate in the War once it starts, but they cannot use Tickets to start one.

## If your Club doesn't have enough Tickets

You cannot start a War that requires more Tickets than your Club has available.

Check the War details for the current requirement and wait until your Club has enough Tickets before starting another challenge.

<Warning>
  Without enough Tickets, your Club cannot start that War challenge.
</Warning>

## War Tickets and Club Wars

War Tickets are only used to start Club Wars. They do not affect how steps are counted or how the winning Club is decided.

For more information, see [How Club Wars work](/clubs-club-wars/how-club-wars-work).
