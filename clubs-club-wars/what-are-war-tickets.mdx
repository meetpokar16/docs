A War Ticket is used by a Club to start a Club War.

Only the Club Leader can use a War Ticket to start a War. The number of Tickets required depends on the type of War.

![Rivlo War tab showing War Tickets status and Club War Ticket artwork](https://rivlo.3bytes.org/guide-assets/war-tickets.png)

## How to start a War with Tickets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the War area">
    Open your Club's **War** area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check requirements">
    Check the available War and its requirements, including your Club's War Tickets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an opponent">
    Choose a Club from the available opponents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the War">
    Start the War. If the War requires a Ticket, it is used when the challenge is started.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the challenge is accepted, the War begins and eligible activity starts counting toward the Club's score.

## How many Tickets do you need?

The Ticket requirement depends on the type of War. Check the War details before starting a challenge.

## Who can use a War Ticket?

Only the **Club Leader** can start a War and use the Club's War Tickets.

Club members can participate once the War starts, but they cannot use Tickets to start one.

## If your Club doesn't have enough Tickets

You cannot start a War that requires more Tickets than your Club has available. Check the War details and wait until your Club has enough Tickets.

## What Tickets do not do

War Tickets are only used to start Club Wars. They do not affect how steps are counted or how the winning Club is decided.

See [How Club Wars work](/clubs-club-wars/how-club-wars-work).
