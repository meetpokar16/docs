title: "How Club Wars Work in Rivlo"
sidebarTitle: "Club Wars"

---

Club Wars are live, time-limited competitions where two clubs go head-to-head using total steps. Every walk, run, and hike logged by your members during the active window adds to your club's combined score — the club with the most steps when the War ends wins. Wars won are tracked on the global leaderboard, so the stakes are real.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter a Live War">
    Open your Club's War area to see everything at a glance: the opposing Club, the War timeline, the current score, and the overall War status. If your Club Leader has used a War Ticket to initiate a challenge against a rival from the Suggested Opponents list, the War will appear here ready to follow. You don't need to do anything to "join" — if you're a Club member, your activity automatically counts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move While the War Is Live">
    Any eligible activity — walks, runs, hikes, or general step tracking — that Rivlo records during the active War window contributes to your Club's total score. The more you move, the more you help. There's no cap on individual contribution, so high-mileage days can make a big difference for the team. Keep an eye on the War timeline so you know how long the window remains open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the Battle">
    Check back on the War area at any time to see the live score gap between the two clubs, a leaderboard of top contributors within your Club, and your own personal step contribution to the team's total. After the War ends, MVP highlights celebrate the members who led the charge — a great motivator to push hard in the final hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Only activity recorded **during the active War window** counts toward your club's score. Steps logged before the War starts or after it ends do not contribute — so stay consistent throughout the entire battle period.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Rivlo uses **anti-cheat protection** to keep competitions fair. Only genuine, device-verified movement counts toward Club War scores. Artificially inflated step counts will not register and may affect your account standing.
</Warning>
