Club Wars are competitions between two Clubs.

During a War, eligible activity recorded by members is added to the Club's total step count. When the War ends, the Club with more steps wins.

## Before a War starts

A Club Leader can start a War by using a War Ticket to challenge another Club.

Once the challenge is accepted and the War begins, members can see the War in their Club's War area.

<Check>
  You do not need to join the War separately. If you are a member of the Club, your eligible activity counts automatically.
</Check>

## During a War

Only activity recorded while the War is active counts toward the Club's score.

This includes eligible steps from:

- Walking
- Running
- Hiking
- General step activity recorded by Rivlo

<Warning>
  Activity recorded before the War starts or after it ends does not count.
</Warning>

There is no individual contribution limit. All eligible activity you record during the War can contribute to your Club's total.

## When the War ends

The Club with the higher total step count wins the War.

The result is recorded after the War ends. Rivlo can also show the members who contributed the most to the winning Club.

## What counts toward a War

Only activity that Rivlo considers eligible is added to a War's score.

Rivlo also uses activity validation to prevent artificial step counts from affecting Club competitions.

<Info>
  If activity is not accepted by Rivlo, it will not be added to the War score.
</Info>

## Club War results

Your Club's War results are recorded in Rivlo so you can keep track of previous competitions.

For information about starting a War, see [What are War Tickets?](/clubs-club-wars/what-are-war-tickets).
