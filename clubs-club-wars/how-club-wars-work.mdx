Club Wars are competitions between two Clubs.

During a War, eligible activity recorded by members is added to the Club's total step count. When the War ends, the Club with more steps wins.

![Active Rivlo Club War showing both Club scores, lead, remaining time, and top performers](https://rivlo.3bytes.org/guide-assets/club-wars.png)

## Before a War starts

A Club Leader can start a War by using a War Ticket to challenge another Club.

Once the challenge is accepted and the War begins, members can see the War in their Club's War area.

You do not need to join the War separately. If you are a Club member, your eligible activity counts automatically.

See [What are War Tickets?](/clubs-club-wars/what-are-war-tickets).

## During a War

Only activity recorded while the War is active counts toward the Club's score. This can include eligible steps from:

- Walking
- Running
- Hiking
- General step activity recorded by Rivlo

Activity recorded before the War starts or after it ends does not count. Prep steps outside the War window do not count.

There is no individual contribution limit. All eligible activity you record during the War can contribute to your Club's total.

## Follow the War

Open the War area to see:

- Both Club scores
- The score difference
- Time remaining
- Your contribution
- Top contributors from your Club

Scores update as eligible activity is recorded.

## When the War ends

The Club with the higher total step count wins. The result is recorded after the War ends. Rivlo can also show the members who contributed the most.

## What counts toward a War

Only activity Rivlo considers eligible is added to a War's score. Rivlo also uses activity validation to prevent artificial step counts from affecting Club competitions.

If activity is not accepted by Rivlo, it is not added to the War score.

## FAQ

### Who can start a Club War?

Only the Club Leader can start a War and use War Tickets.

### What activity counts?

Eligible activity recorded during the active War window. Exact eligibility is determined by Rivlo's activity validation.

### What happens when a War ends?

The Club with the higher total wins, and Rivlo records the result.
