Clubs are groups of Rivlo players who compete together.

You can join an existing Club or create your own. A Club can have up to **50 members**.

Your Club has its own members, leaderboard, activity feed, and chat. Clubs can also compete against other Clubs in Club Wars.

## Joining a Club

You can browse Clubs that are open for new members and request to join them. You can also join through an invitation from an existing member.

Once you join, your activity is included in the Club's leaderboard and other Club features.

## Creating a Club

When you create a Club, you become the **Leader**. Leaders can:

- Manage Club members
- Send and manage invites
- Assign Club roles
- Remove members
- Start Club Wars

## Club roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Leader** | Full Club management, including starting Club Wars |
| **Co-Leader** | Helps manage the Club and day-to-day Club activity |

## Club activity

- **Leaderboard**: ranks members by activity
- **Activity feed**: recent activity from Club members
- **Club Chat**: messaging between members

Your activity contributes to the Club's results when you take part in Club competitions.

## Club Wars

During a Club War, members contribute steps to the Club's total score. The two Clubs are compared based on their scores.

See [How Club Wars work](/clubs-club-wars/how-club-wars-work) and [What are War Tickets?](/clubs-club-wars/what-are-war-tickets).

## What you can do as a member

- Track your position on the Club leaderboard
- See other members' activity
- Use Club Chat
- Participate in Club Wars
- Contribute activity to your Club's score
