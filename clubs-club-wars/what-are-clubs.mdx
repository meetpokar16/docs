Clubs are groups of Rivlo players who compete together.

You can join an existing Club or create your own. A Club can have up to **50 members**.

Your Club has its own members, leaderboard, activity feed, and chat. Clubs can also compete against other Clubs in Club Wars.

## Joining a Club

You can browse Clubs that are open for new members and request to join them.

You can also join a Club through an invitation from an existing member.

Once you join, your activity is included in the Club's leaderboard and other Club features.

## Creating a Club

You can create your own Club instead of joining an existing one.

When you create a Club, you become the **Leader**. Leaders can:

- Manage Club members
- Send and manage invites
- Assign Club roles
- Remove members
- Start Club Wars

## Club roles

Clubs have two leadership roles:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Leader" icon="crown">
    The Leader manages the Club and has full access to Club management features, including starting Club Wars.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Co-Leader" icon="user-shield">
    A Co-Leader helps manage the Club and can help with day-to-day Club activity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Club Wars

Clubs can compete against other Clubs in **Club Wars**.

During a War, members contribute their steps to the Club's total score. The two Clubs are then compared based on their scores.

See [How Club Wars work](/clubs-club-wars/how-club-wars-work) to learn how Wars are started and scored.

## What you can do in a Club

As a Club member, you can:

- Track your position on the Club leaderboard
- See other members' activity
- Use Club Chat
- Participate in Club Wars
- Contribute activity to your Club's score
