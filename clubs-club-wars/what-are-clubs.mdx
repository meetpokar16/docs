title: "What Are Clubs in Rivlo?"
sidebarTitle: "What Are Clubs?"
description: "Clubs are Rivlo's team feature — join an existing group or create your own to move together, track contributions, and compete in Club Wars."
---

Clubs are Rivlo teams built around shared movement. You can join an existing club that fits your pace and style, or create your own for the people you already move with. Up to 50 members can belong to a single club, making every walk, run, or hike feel like a team effort.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find or Create a Club">
    Browse open Clubs and join one that matches your goals, or start a brand-new Club from scratch. When you create a Club, you become its Leader — giving you control over membership, settings, and the ability to kick off Club Wars. Open Clubs are discoverable by any Rivlo user, so your roster can grow organically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the Team">
    Invite members directly using Club Invites or let people discover and request to join. Once your crew is in, use Club Chat to coordinate and keep the energy going. The Club's activity feed lets every member see how individual contributions stack up — the leaderboard updates as steps roll in, so there's always something to race toward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take on Bigger Goals">
    When a Club War is active, every member's movement counts toward the team's total score. Clubs compete head-to-head against a rival club, pooling their steps into a single combined effort. The more active your members, the stronger your Club's position — consistent participation from even the quietest members can swing a close War.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Clubs have two leadership roles: **Leader** and **Co-Leader**. Leaders manage membership (invites, removals, and role assignments) and can initiate Club Wars. Co-Leaders can help keep the club active and support day-to-day coordination.
</Tip>
