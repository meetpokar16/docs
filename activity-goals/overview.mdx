## Daily step goals

Set a daily step target and track your progress throughout the day.

Rivlo updates your step count as new activity is recorded. Your Home screen shows how close you are to reaching your goal.

<Card title="Home screen" icon="house" href="/getting-started/understanding-home-screen">
  See how daily goal progress appears on Home.
</Card>

## Streaks

A streak counts the consecutive days on which you reach your daily step goal.

Rivlo shows your current streak and your longest streak.

<Tip>
  Hitting your daily goal each day is what keeps a streak alive.
</Tip>

## Distance and calories

Rivlo can show estimated distance and calories alongside your step count.

These values are based on the activity data available to Rivlo and may differ from measurements from other apps or devices.

<Info>
  For how Rivlo reads activity data, see [How Rivlo works — What you need to use Rivlo](/getting-started/how-rivlo-works#what-you-need-to-use-rivlo).
</Info>

## Body goals

You can set a body goal in Rivlo and use it alongside your activity goals.

Your body goal can affect the activity targets Rivlo recommends.

## Run tracking

Rivlo can record runs using GPS.

A recorded run can include:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Distance" icon="ruler-horizontal">
    How far you ran.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Pace" icon="gauge-high">
    How fast you moved overall.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Splits" icon="timeline">
    Segment-by-segment performance.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Route" icon="route">
    The path GPS recorded for the run.
  </Card>
</Columns>

After a run, you can review the recorded activity in Rivlo.

## Hike tracking

Rivlo can also record hikes using GPS.

Depending on your device and the data available, a hike can include:

- Distance
- Pace
- Route
- Elevation
- Altitude

## Rivlo Adventures

Rivlo Adventures lets you follow virtual hiking routes using your real-world steps.

As you walk, your progress is applied to the selected route.
