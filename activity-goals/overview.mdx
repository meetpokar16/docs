Rivlo tracks your steps, runs, hikes, and goals — giving you a clear picture of your progress each day and over time. Whether you're chasing a daily step goal, building a streak, or mapping a trail run, everything feeds into a single activity view that grows with you.

## What Activity & Goals Covers

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Daily Step Goals" icon="shoe-prints">
    Set a personal step target and track your progress throughout the day. Rivlo updates your count in real time and notifies you when you're close to hitting your goal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Streaks" icon="fire">
    Keep your momentum going by hitting your daily goal every day. Rivlo tracks your current streak and your longest streak so you always have something to protect.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Distance & Calories" icon="person-walking">
    Alongside steps, Rivlo estimates the distance you've covered and the calories you've burned — giving you a fuller picture of each day's effort.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Body Goals" icon="heart-pulse">
    Set a weight-loss goal and let Rivlo factor it into your daily activity targets, keeping your movement aligned with your bigger health picture.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Run Tracking" icon="person-running">
    Track runs with splits, distance, pace, and route mapping. Every run is logged so you can review your performance and spot improvements over time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hike Tracking" icon="mountain">
    Record hikes with GPS, pace, elevation, and altitude data. Ideal for trail sessions where vertical gain matters as much as distance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rivlo Adventures" icon="map">
    Take on Virtual Hikes through Rivlo Adventures — guided routes that overlay your real-world steps onto iconic trails around the world.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pro Analytics" icon="chart-line">
    Rivlo Pro unlocks hourly step tracking, Activity with Time analytics, weekly and monthly and yearly insights, and yearly heatmaps to reveal your long-term patterns.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
