## Distance and calories

Rivlo can show estimated distance and calories alongside your step count. These values are based on the activity data available to Rivlo and may differ from other apps or devices.

## Body goals

You can set a body goal in Rivlo and use it alongside your activity goals. Your body goal can affect the activity targets Rivlo recommends.

## Rivlo Adventures

Rivlo Adventures lets you follow virtual hiking routes using your real-world steps. As you walk, your progress is applied to the selected route.

## Analytics

Rivlo provides activity analytics so you can review activity beyond today's step count.

Rivlo Pro includes additional analytics, such as:

- Hourly step data
- Activity by time
- Weekly, monthly, and yearly views
- Yearly heatmaps
