Rivlo records your daily steps so you can see how much you move each day.

## How steps enter Rivlo

Depending on your device and setup, Rivlo can use your health or activity data automatically:

- **iPhone**: Apple Health
- **Android**: Health Connect

You can also record supported activities manually when that option is available.

See [How Rivlo gets your activity data](/getting-started/activity-data).

## Where steps appear

Your current step count appears on the Home screen. Rivlo also keeps previous activity so you can review how you have been doing over time.

The same step data can contribute to:

- Daily goal progress
- Streaks
- Leaderboards
- Club results
- Club War scores
- League progression

## Related measurements

Rivlo can also show estimated distance and calories alongside your step count. These values are based on the activity data available to Rivlo and may differ from other apps or devices.
