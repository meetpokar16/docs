You can set a daily step goal in Rivlo and track progress toward it throughout the day.

![Rivlo screen showing daily goal and streak](/images/set-goals.png)

## Daily step goals

Your goal is the number of steps you set as your daily target. Rivlo updates your step count as new activity is recorded. The Home screen shows how close you are to your goal.

Your goal is personal. You can change it as your activity changes.

## Streaks

A streak counts the consecutive days on which you reach your daily step goal.

Rivlo shows:

- Your **current streak**
- Your **longest streak**

If you miss your goal for a day, that breaks the consecutive-day streak.

## Where to see progress

You can see goal progress and streak information on the Home screen. See [Understanding the Home screen](/getting-started/understanding-home-screen).
