Rivlo can record runs using GPS.

A recorded run can include:

- Distance
- Pace
- Splits
- Route

After a run, you can review the recorded activity in Rivlo.

Eligible run activity recorded during an active Club War can contribute to your Club's War score. See [How Club Wars work](/clubs-club-wars/how-club-wars-work).
