Rivlo can record hikes using GPS.

Depending on your device and the data available, a hike can include:

- Distance
- Pace
- Route
- Elevation
- Altitude

After a hike, you can review the recorded activity in Rivlo.

Eligible hike activity recorded during an active Club War can contribute to your Club's War score. See [How Club Wars work](/clubs-club-wars/how-club-wars-work).
